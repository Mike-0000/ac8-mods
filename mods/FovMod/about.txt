Set your own field of view for the cockpit, HUD-only and third-person cameras of
ACE COMBAT 8. Change it while you fly; each view remembers its own value.

IN GAME
  =  and  -    widen / narrow the view you are in by 5 degrees
  F10          switch the mod on or off
  F9           reload fov.ini

Nothing changes until you press = in flight or edit the settings.
The game's own values: Cockpit 73.74, HUD 80, Third Person 61.93.

SETTINGS
  In the FovMod folder, copy defaults.ini to fov.ini and edit the copy.
  Values are 40 to 150 degrees, or 0 to leave a view to the game. Keys and step size
  are set there too; key changes need a game restart.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - The HUD overlay does not scale with the field of view, so the pitch ladder no
    longer lines up exactly with the world at other values.
  - The mod only acts in the three flight views. Menus and cutscenes are left alone.
  - Do not run it together with another FOV mod: two mods writing the camera angle
    make this one stand down for the session.
  - Log: fovmod.log in the FovMod folder.
