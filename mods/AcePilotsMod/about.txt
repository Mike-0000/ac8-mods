Makes enemy pilots in ACE COMBAT 8 fly and fight harder, with an in-game menu to
choose how much. Everything is put back when a setting is turned off.

IN GAME
  F6    open the menu (mouse, arrow keys or gamepad)
  F8    step through the presets: Off, All rank 5, Boosted 2, Boosted 3, Boosted 4, MAX

WHAT YOU CAN CHANGE
  Pilot skill   pilot rank for every enemy (5 is a named ace), how often they pick the
                manoeuvres that better pilots use, how rarely they pick the lazy ones
  Aircraft      turn rates and acceleration, missiles in the air per enemy, how long
                they hold a hard turn before blacking out
  Aggression    no waiting for a turn to attack, flares, evasion and taunt manoeuvres
                allowed even where the mission had switched them off
  Applies to    aircraft only or ground and sea units too, allies too, and from which
                game difficulty the manoeuvre odds are changed
  Behaviour     chance that a fighter type gets a harder behaviour script

SETTINGS
  acepilots.ini in the AcePilotsMod folder. The mod writes it on first start and
  rewrites it when you change something in the menu. Edits made in the file are
  picked up within a second.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Until acepilots.ini exists the mod starts on the "All rank 5" preset.
  - Agility above 2 can make enemies overshoot.
  - Built for Steam build 25201480. On any other build the native part finds that the
    game code has changed and stays off; pilot rank and the aggression switches still work.
  - Log: acepilots.log in the AcePilotsMod folder.
